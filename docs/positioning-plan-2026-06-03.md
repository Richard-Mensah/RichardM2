## 3. Information architecture — from sprawl to spine

**Current:** 7 top-level items; Research has 10 children, Projects 7; heavy overlap
(AI & Data Science appears under both Research and Projects); "KOFIEVER" is opaque;
~50 routes, many thin.

**Proposed top nav (6 items, self-evident labels):**

```
HOME
ABOUT          → Profile (credentials) · Biography · Leadership journey · CV
WORK           → merges Research + Projects, organised by the 3 pillars:
                   · AI & Data for Development
                   · Climate Intelligence
                   · Youth & Community Impact
                   · Writing & Working Papers
WORLD STAGE    → Conferences & Summits · Media & Speaking · Collaborations
KOFIEVER       → keep, but relabel in nav as "OPPORTUNITIES (Kofiever)"
CONTACT
```

(SDGs and Gallery become sections/links within the above rather than top-level
peers — they support the story, they aren't the headline.)

**Why:** a panel can traverse the whole story in 6 confident clicks instead of
hunting through 17 dropdown items, and every landing page is full, not a stub.

**Thin-page policy:** any route that can't be filled with real, specific content
gets merged or removed before launch. An empty "Research Papers" page is worse
than no page.
